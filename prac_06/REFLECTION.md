# CP1404 Practical Reflection


## What have you changed about your process in this subject based on your personal reflection?

Before I code the whole answer, I divide challenges down into smaller, testable components. I now spend more time 
planning, figuring out what I need to do, and sketching out the structure before I start writing the code. This has 
helped cut down on mistakes and made it easier to find bugs.

## What do you still need to change in terms of your approach, application, time on task, attitude, etc.?

I still need to be better at managing my time. I often wait until close to the due date to do my prac exercises, which 
makes me feel more stressed and less able to learn. I also need to take more time to look over feedback and make 
changes based on it instead of just moving on to the next task.

## What are you doing well in the subject so far that you will keep doing?


I've been doing all of the practical tasks on time and trying to figure out the reasoning behind each idea. When I'm 
stuck, I also ask for help instead of getting stuck for too long. I also aim to keep getting better at using version 
control and PyCharm.

## What do you need to stop or change in your work for this subject overall so far?

I need to stop racing through things just to get them done. I want to put more effort into developing code that is 
clean, easy to read, and well-documented. I also need to avoid skipping optional parts because they often have good 
practice in them.

## What is one request you have or change you suggest for this subject and/or your IT degree overall?

It would be helpful to have brief video summaries for each practical that go over the main ideas and mistakes that 
people make a lot. Sometimes written instructions are too much to handle or are hard to understand. Adding a little 
more real-world context to jobs would also make them more interesting to learn.
